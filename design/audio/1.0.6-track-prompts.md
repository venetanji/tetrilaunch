# Three beds for 1.0.6 — Suno prompt sheet

**Read this in one sitting, generate in one sitting.** Three beds, three takes
each. The winner goes into `audio/tracks/` under **its own song title** — beds
are the one family that keeps the generator's name, because a title is a real
thing and a ROLE is an assignment that can change (`audio/README.md`).

- Masters folder: `audio/` at the repo root. **Gitignored, and main-checkout
  only** — a worktree cannot run `audio:prepare` until the masters are copied in.
- `audio:prepare` **deletes `app/public/audio/` before rebuilding**. Never run it
  on a checkout without `audio/fx`, `audio/stingers` and `audio/tracks`
  populated: it would unship the whole soundtrack and cannot rebuild it.
- The runbook for the session these three are generated in is at the bottom.

---

## The gap these three fill

The ladder is finished: ten bays, ten songs, and `sim/systems.ts` pins that no
two of them collide and that the arc never runs backwards. Everything **else**
in the game shared **one** bed. `menu` played the front door, the tier hub, the
Workshop, the Contract board, the leaderboard, the pause card, the tutorial's
failures and the seal notice — and it also plays the store trailer's title cards
(`sim/promo/timeline.json`). One lounge track, written to sit under a paused
bay, carrying every surface a player is on when they are not flying.

So the out-of-bay half gets an arc too, split by what the player is DOING:

| role | plays over | what it has to say |
|---|---|---|
| `theme` | the front door (`menuScreen`, PR #223), `/about`, the promo cut's title beats | this is the game |
| `hub` | the tier hub (`"tiers"`), Workshop, leaderboard | you are choosing what to fly |
| `contracts` | the Contract board | the day's three jobs |
| `menu` | pause, Settings, tutorial fail, the seal notice | nothing is happening right now |

`menu` keeps the job it was always good at and loses the four it was never
written for. **Nothing is being re-scored** — no existing master moves.

### What was considered and NOT given a track

- **The tier-unlock ceremony.** It already has its own voice: `unlockFanfare`, a
  stinger, shipped. The rainbow **hold** on the Unlock button is a different cue
  again and also already exists (`holdCharge`). Nothing to generate.
- **The Skydeck.** The roof genuinely borrows the whole ladder — bay 1 to bay 10
  again — and that is a real gap. It is not a ONE-track gap: a Skydeck run is
  `RUN_LEVELS` bays long, so a single roof bed would be twenty minutes of one
  loop in the one run with no retry. The cheaper fix is run design (start the
  roof's ladder deeper) and it costs no audio at all. Left open.
- **The seal notice.** `seal-break` drops to the lounge deliberately: the player
  is reading a price, and a cue under a decision is the game arguing for one of
  the answers (`main.ts`'s `syncMusic`). `sealBreak` marks the press itself.
- **A promo-only track.** `sim/systems.ts` fails on a music file no role claims
  ("no music file ships unclaimed"), so a bed that exists purely for the trailer
  is a red build. `theme` is the trailer's piece *because* it has a screen.
- **The Contract tier window's clamp** (tiers 8, 9 and 10 all deal bays 8–10).
  That is the ladder ending at ten, not a missing song; it wants bays 11–13.

## House style, for all three

The twelve shipped beds are in twelve unrecorded keys and nothing documents
their tempos, so the only style facts worth stating are the ones the files
themselves prove: the ladder runs *chill* (`chill beginning`, `2 chill`) through
*circuit/disco* (`raggae circuit`, `Chipdisco`) into *neon* (`Neon Circuit`,
`Neon Static`, `Neon Pixel Pulse`), and the out-of-bay bed is a lounge
(`lounge-menu-pause`). These three sit in that world: **neon-arcade electronic,
instrumental, no vocals.**

Five rules that come from the pipeline rather than from taste:

1. **No fade in, no fade out.** `el.loop = true` plays the whole file end to
   end, so the master's first bar and last bar ARE the loop seam. A take that
   opens on a swell and closes on a decay puts a dip in every cycle. Ask for a
   clean top-of-bar start and a hard stop.
2. **Keep the energy above 500 Hz.** The pipeline measures a phone-band spread
   across the beds and **fails the run** past 3 dB (`PHONE_SPREAD_DB`). `bay-1`
   needed a permanent `MASTER_EQ` entry — a −9 dB shelf at 220 Hz plus a
   limiter — because 72% of its loudness sat under 200 Hz, where a phone speaker
   moves no air. A sub-heavy take is not a loud take, it is a silent one.
3. **Steady level, not a build.** Every long-form asset is loudness-normalised
   to −15 LUFS with `LRA` 11, and `loudnorm` quietly switches from linear to
   dynamic compression if the input range exceeds that — which the run **also**
   fails. `contractClear` is the cautionary entry in `MASTER_EQ`. Ask for even
   dynamics: no drop, no breakdown, no silence.
4. **2–3 minutes, not 30 seconds.** The shipped beds run 105–185 s at 128 kbps
   (1.7–2.9 MB each). Three more beds add roughly 6–8 MB to a 32 MB audio folder
   that is entirely PWA-precached, so stay at the short end of that window
   unless the piece earns the length.
5. **Audition against the screen, not in silence** — and for `theme`,
   specifically against the live demo, which has its own effects playing over it.

# `theme` — the front door

**One-line brief:** *Instrumental neon-arcade synthwave title theme, ~104 BPM,
confident mid-tempo, one clear four-bar hook stated in the first eight bars,
bright analog lead over a steady pulse, even dynamics, no vocals, no build or
drop, starts cold on the downbeat and ends hard — loopable, 100–120 s.*

**Why it is first.** This is the calling card. It plays over the live demo
behind the front door, it is what the `/about` page should carry, and it is what
the store cut opens and closes on — `timeline.json`'s two `title` beats play
`audio/music/menu.mp3` today, i.e. the game currently introduces itself with the
track written to sit under a paused bay.

**Judge takes by the first eight bars,** because the trailer's title cards are
3.0 s long: the hook has to have arrived by then. Then check it still works as
two minutes of wallpaper behind the demo — a theme that is exhausting on the
third loop is the wrong take, however good the opening is.

---

# `hub` — the tier hub

**One-line brief:** *Instrumental neon-arcade electronic bed, ~100 BPM,
arpeggio-led and forward-moving but unobtrusive, no lead melody, warm pads over
a light 4/4 pulse, completely even dynamics, no vocals, no build or drop, clean
loop with no fade at either end, 120–150 s.*

**Why.** After PR #223 this is where the out-of-run minutes actually go: picking
a tier on the tierlevator, reading the bay recap, walking to the Workshop and
back. It is a room you stand in, not a moment — so it wants motion without a
statement. **No hook**, deliberately: `theme` is one screen away and two hooks
back to back is one hook too many.

The Workshop and the leaderboard take this bed as well, so it has to hold up
under a screen someone is reading numbers on.

---

# `contracts` — the Contract board

**One-line brief:** *Instrumental downtempo neon-arcade bed, ~84 BPM, sparse and
patient, one repeating motif with a dry ticking pulse under it, minor and
unresolved, no lead melody, even dynamics, no vocals, no build or drop, clean
loop with no fade at either end, 100–130 s.*

**Why.** The board is a daily ritual — three cards, once a day, gone tomorrow —
and it is the one out-of-run screen with a clock on it. Slower and sparser than
`hub` on purpose: the player is comparing three jobs, not moving.

**It must not sound like a bay.** The moment a Contract actually STARTS, the bed
switches to a bay bed on loan — `contractBed` deals the slot's bed in the tier
window, bay 5's 5/4 track for a pentomino board, or the 1-in-20 `contract-rare`.
The board's own bed is the thing you leave when the job begins, so the contrast
at that transition is the point.

# Runbook — the session that lands these

Everything below happens in the **main checkout**, where the masters live. A
worktree cannot run the pipeline.

1. **Generate**, three takes each, to the briefs above. Audition against the
   screens, not in silence.

2. **Name the winners.** Beds keep their song titles — drop them in
   `audio/tracks/` as e.g. `Front Door.mp3`, `Tierlevator.mp3`, `Day Job.mp3`.
   The title is what goes in `MUSIC`; the ROLE is what the code sees.

3. **Map them**, in `app/scripts/prepare-audio.mjs`:
   ```js
   const MUSIC = {
     ...
     "Front Door.mp3": "theme",
     "Tierlevator.mp3": "hub",
     "Day Job.mp3": "contracts",
   };
   ```
   …and **delete those three names from `PENDING_MUSIC`** in the same pass. The
   run fails if a role is in both, and the systems harness fails if a pending
   role has shipped — two sides of the same reminder.

4. **Run it:** `cd app && npm run audio:prepare` (needs `ffmpeg` **and**
   `ffprobe`). Read four things in the output:
   - the three new `music` rows: `src → -15.0 LUFS  TP -x.x dB  phone -xx.x
     linear`. **`linear` is not optional.** Anything reporting `dynamic` means
     `loudnorm` compressed on its own initiative and the run fails; the fix is a
     declared `acompressor`/`alimiter` entry in `MASTER_EQ`, not a re-render.
   - `long-form spread`, which must stay inside 1.0 LU across every bed and
     stinger.
   - `phone-band spread`, which must stay inside 3.0 dB **across the beds**. A
     new bed that lands 4 dB under the rest is DARK, not quiet — give it a
     `MASTER_EQ` shelf like `bay-1`'s and re-run.
   - `total shipped`, which should rise by 6–8 MB and no more.

   `npm run audio:prepare -- --compare` renders the whole codec matrix into
   `audio/compare/` from the masters and ships nothing; it is only worth the
   minutes if the new beds are being used to revisit the codec choice.

5. **Check the loop seams by ear.** The pipeline does not trim beds, so the seam
   is the master's own two ends. Let each new bed run past its loop point twice
   on the test phone. A dip or a thump at the seam is a re-generation, not a
   fix — there is no trim window for beds.

6. **Route the screens** in `app/src/main.ts`'s `syncMusic` — the half this
   branch deliberately left out, because a screen pointed at a bed that does not
   exist is a SILENT screen (`playMusic` swallows the 404 by design). It is four
   one-line moves: the front door plays `"theme"`, the `"tiers"` hub plays
   `"hub"`, `"contracts"` plays `"contracts"`, and pause/Settings/`coach-fail`/
   `exam-fail`/`seal-break` keep `"menu"`. Do it **after PR #223 merges**, since
   the hub state arrives with it.

7. **Validate:** `npm run typecheck && npm test && npm run test:uifit && npm run
   build`, from `app/`. uifit must report 0 new.

8. **Commit** `app/public/audio/music/*.mp3` together with the `MUSIC` /
   `PENDING_MUSIC` / `syncMusic` edits. The shipped assets are the half that
   ships — CI has no guaranteed `ffmpeg`, and they are what `cap sync` copies.
   Quote the LUFS / phone-band numbers the run printed in the commit message.
