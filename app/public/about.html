Part Tetris line-clearer, part Angry-Birds trajectory launcher — dressed as a space-logistics roguelite of cannons, cargo and salvage.

How it plays

1

Aim

Drag back from the cannon — direction sets the angle, distance sets the power. A dotted parabola shows exactly where the piece will fly.

2

Launch

Release to fire. Tetrominoes arc across the bay under real physics and settle wherever they land — so every shot has to count.

3

Clear

A row clears only when it's completely filled. Feed full rows to the sweeping compactor and bank your funding before the clock runs out.

Two ways to fly

DEEP RUN

The gauntlet

Ten bays, one life. Each bay you clear lets you ratchet one axis of difficulty and refit your ship with salvage. Ten tiers, each with its own leaderboard — how deep can you get?

CONTRACTS

Quick challenges

Short, self-contained bays you can retry as much as you like. A fresh set every day from a shared seed — including a zero-waste pattern contract for the perfectionists.
