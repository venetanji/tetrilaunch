Support

Tetrilaunch is a physics cannon puzzle: you fire tetrominoes from a cannon, stack what lands, and clear lines to fulfil contracts. It plays in landscape on phones, tablets and in the browser.

Common questions

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device only — there is no account and no cloud save. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, erases it permanently. In a browser, note that "private" or "incognito" windows discard storage when you close them, and some browsers clear site data automatically after a period of inactivity.

I bought the Full Game but it isn't showing

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with the store receipt and we'll sort it out.

What does the Full Game purchase unlock?

It is a one-time purchase: it opens earned Tiers 4–10 and gives unlimited, on-demand Contracts. It does not skip progression or unlock the Skydeck; those are still earned by playing. Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies, but tell us what happened and we'll help.

The game is stuck in portrait

Tetrilaunch is landscape-only. If it won't rotate, check that your device's rotation lock is off.

Can I get my leaderboard entry removed?

Yes. Email us the display name, the score and roughly when you set it, and we'll delete it. See the privacy policy for what the leaderboard stores.

Reporting a bug

What helps most:

  • your device and OS version (e.g. "Pixel 8, Android 15")
  • whether it's the app or the browser version
  • what you were doing when it happened, and whether it happens every time
  • a screenshot or screen recording if you can
